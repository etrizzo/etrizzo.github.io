Ceramics

I have been studying ceramics since 2021, primarily focusing on wheel throwing and making functional objects such as mugs, planters, vases, jars, and cookware. I love highlighting the texture and color of the clay, while also experimenting with fluid, dynamic glazing techniques. I enjoy incorporating my illustrations into my work through underglaze painting and carving techniques such as sgraffito.

Stoneware vase with marbled "agateware" technique using the white clay body stained with red iron oxide. The design is carved into the clay, and decorated with cobalt, copper, manganese, and iron oxides. The fish illustration and vase decoration is inspired by work of the artist Jirō Kinjō, the first "Living National Treasure" from Okinawa.

This work was made as a gift for my grandmother, who grew up on the nearby island of Ishigaki and spent many years on Okinawa before coming to America.
